PB Sales App privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

PB Sales App is an internal tool of Plates & Buckets. It is used only by the restaurant owner and staff.

What it uses

The app runs as a Google Apps Script project under the restaurant owner's Google account. With the owner's permission it reads and writes the restaurant's own Google Sheet (sales, closing, market orders and stock), sends alert emails from that account to the owner, and updates its own Apps Script code and deployment.

What it does not do

It does not collect data from members of the public, does not sell or share any data, does not use data for advertising, and does not send data to anyone other than the restaurant owner. Google user data is used only to run the app.
